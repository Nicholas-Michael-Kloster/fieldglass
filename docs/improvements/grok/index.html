System Profile & Design North Star

North Star: Separate primary timestamped evidence from social pulse via Dual-Cone Causal Chiasm, spectral bot de-echoing, and closed-loop verification.

Observed Strengths

  • Ultra-low-latency ingestion of live X social stream alongside web and multimodal feeds.
  • High responsiveness on breaking events, emerging developer chatter, and real-time trends.
  • Direct, conversational tone adaptable to technical debugging and rapid synthesis.

Core Architectural Bottlenecks

  • Live RAG blends unverified viral rumors, satire, and primary advisories into one voice.
  • Coordinated bot-echo copy-pasta and stale high-like posts distort naive engagement ranking.
  • Charts/screenshots and technical claims lack automated pixel-level and sandbox verification.

GROK-01 · Dual-Cone Primary-Source Splitter & Domain Time-Decay Index

P0 Near-term (0-3 mo) Temporal Primary Grounding
Problem / Gap

Real-time retrieval conflates viral jokes and rumors with primary engineering/CVE advisories, while older high-like posts outrank newer official corrections.

Architectural Proposal

Maintain separate PrimaryEvidenceCone and SocialPulseCone indexes with domain-calibrated exponential freshness decay lambda_domain and SUPERSEDED_BY_T+delta tags; evaluate cones via causal_chiasm() and surface a ?? Split-View Caution Badge when EI < 0.25.

Fieldglass Grounding

Uses causal_chiasm(primary_cone, social_cone) Effective Information (EI) and ?? glyph to flag uncorroborated viral divergence.

Concrete Deliverable

SocialVsPrimaryGate ranker + TimeDecayIndex freshness scorer with explicit conflict badge.
